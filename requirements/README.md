## Proposal content and process

Source: proposal form, Instructions and report fields.

- Identification, supervisor, title, major, date and venue; select actual project source.
- Literature/background section: frontier, current status, challenges and significance;
  **at least 1,000 English words for master's students in this section**, not an explicit
  minimum for the entire proposal. Chinese text specifies 5,000 Chinese characters.
- Mark citations in the text and list them in order at the end of that section. Complete
  reference metadata is required; the form permits different reference formats.
- Research objectives, contents and key issues; methods, scheme and feasibility;
  novelty; schedule and expected outcomes; prior work and actual achievements.
- A4, left binding, additional pages permitted. Send the printed form to supervisor
  and reviewers at least one week before the oral presentation (15 October for a
  22 October defense). Confirm local physical-delivery arrangements.
- Apply/upload online; exception needs Graduate School consent. Archive the approved
  final version with student and supervisor; Chinese instructions say no re-upload needed.
- Do not reproduce sample dates, signatures or degree-program labels as personal facts.
  The Chinese novelty-search instruction applies to doctoral students. The Chinese
  committee instruction is specifically for equivalent-academic-qualification students;
  do not infer a universal MIB committee rule from the broader English translation.

Slide 2 of the defense template suggests background, research question, literature,
research plan/method, expected results/innovation, challenges/limitations and progress.
Its sample name/date are placeholders; five supplied slides are not a stated slide limit.
No presentation duration is specified in these files.

## Final thesis formatting

Source: formatting guidelines, basic requirements and sections II–III.

- English main body, **at least 15,000 words**. This is a thesis requirement, not the proposal minimum.
- Main body: Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.25 spacing, footnotes. General printing section
  also permits double spacing; use the specific main-body instruction unless clarified.
- A4, left binding; title page unnumbered; preliminary pages lowercase Roman numerals,
  body/appendices/bibliography Arabic numerals.
- Order: cover/title, originality statement, authorization statement, English abstract,
  contents, main body, references, appendices, acknowledgements. English abstract no
  more than 1,500 words; Chinese abstract optional in the example contents.
- Chicago reference guidance, superscript citations and reference endnotes; explanatory
  annotations use footnotes. Clarify exact implementation if needed rather than silently
  switching to author-date. Proposal form has its own more flexible reference instruction.
- Consecutive Arabic numbering for figures/tables/notes/formulas; figure captions below,
  table captions above. Appendix sample typography is retained in the original file.
- Cover examples use Business Administration and 2019 dates: verify correct MIB degree
  wording and actual defense date rather than copying those examples.

## Midterm

Source: midterm form, report field: at least **3,200 English words** on progress,
achievements and next steps, plus an accurate list of publications/other achievements.
No achievements should be invented to fill the form. Generic form instructions mention
an oral presentation and advance print review, but the cohort notice explicitly removes
in-person midterm defense. Confirm any remaining print requirement with Teaching Office.
